# ✦ Gloamwick™ Potion Sort

*A game by Briarlune Studios.*

A cozy, witchy sand-sorting puzzle game. Pour glittering enchanted sands across five professors' classrooms, each with its own potions, ambience and signature spell.

**Play free:** https://briarlunestudios.github.io/gloamwick/

## The classrooms

| Classroom | Professor | Signature spell |
|---|---|---|
| Moonlit Glasshouse | Wren Hollowmere, Herbalism | **Sprout**: grows an extra empty vial |
| Observatory Spire | Orrin Vesper, Astronomy | **Starsight**: reveals every hidden layer |
| Ember Forge | Bastian Cinderhall, Alchemy | **Distill**: makes the next right pour for you |
| Tea-Leaf Parlor | Madame Seraphine Lune, Divination | **Foretell**: points to the best next pour |
| Hushed Crypt | Corvin Ashgrave, Shadow Arts | **Unbind**: your next pour ignores the color rule |

30 levels that get harder brew by brew inside every classroom: more colors, trickier starting mixes, longer solves, and fewer undos as you go. Finish a classroom to unlock its **bonus brew**: harder, fully veiled, and worth double points. Every level is checked to be solvable.

## Spellcraft and academy years

Every potion you bottle goes onto your shelf. In the grimoire's **Spellcraft** page, combine discovered ingredients and bottled potions in the cauldron to craft spells that help in every brew for good: an extra undo, a self-stirring spoon, a charm that lifts the veil, and more.

Craft all of your year's **exam spells** to move up a year. Your Second Year brings new, harder brews to all five classrooms. In your Third Year the vials come in three sizes: tiny 3-layer vials, standard vials and great 5-layer flasks, with colors that come in matching amounts.

## Familiars

Cash in points to adopt a familiar. Choose a kind (cat, dragon, owl, rat, frog, snake, spider or a little tree), then one of three companions of that kind, and give them a name. Feed it treats to grow your bond. As you grow closer it starts pointing out pours, up to two hints a brew that no longer cost you stars. Each brew empties one of its bowls, so keep it fed.

## Achievements and lore

Earn achievements for progress, skill and collecting. Each one pays out tokens to spend in the grimoire on Pocket Sprouts (an extra empty vial in any brew) and sealed lore pages about the Academy and its professors.

Score points for every potion bottled, with bonuses for swift brewing and for brewing without help. A short how-to-play guide greets new witches (tap **?** to see it again). Progress saves in your browser.

## How it's built

One self-contained `index.html`. No install, no account.
